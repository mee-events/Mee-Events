How quotation approval and booking confirmation work

Understanding the booking process ensures your event planning remains seamless. Once you've finalized your requirements with our planning team, we initiate a clear, 4-step confirmation process.

1

Quotation Sent

A detailed, itemized quotation is generated by your event manager and sent to your registered email. This document outlines all services, taxes, and timelines.

2

Customer Review

You have up to 72 hours to review the document. During this phase, you can request revisions or additions through the Mee Events portal.

3

Approval

Once satisfied, digitally sign the quotation to formally approve the scope of work. This locks in your preferred dates and vendor availability.

4

Payment Verification

Submit the advance payment as stipulated in the contract. Upon successful verification, your booking status changes to 'Confirmed'.
