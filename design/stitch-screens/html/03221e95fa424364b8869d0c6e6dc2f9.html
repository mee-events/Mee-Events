How the Booking Confirmation Process Works

Understanding the steps from receiving your initial quotation to final confirmation of your event planning services with Mee Events.

1

Quotation Sent

You will receive a versioned quotation outlining the detailed scope of services, comprehensive pricing, applicable taxes, quotation validity period, and specific payment terms. This document serves as the foundation for your event plan.

2

Customer Review

Review the quotation thoroughly. This is the stage to request any clarifications or revisions to ensure the proposed scope perfectly aligns with your vision before proceeding to approval.

3

Approval

Provide your explicit acknowledgement and approval of the quotation. Please note that this step signifies agreement to the terms and scope but does not complete the payment process.

4

Payment Verification

Your booking is confirmed only after the initial payment is successfully processed and verified by our system.

info

Important Note

Changes to the approved scope may require a new quotation version to be issued and approved.
