Stage Concept

A tiered, architectural backdrop featuring interwoven floral arches and integrated LED uplighting to create a sense of depth and grandeur while maintaining a modern, clean silhouette.

Seating Direction

Lounge-style groupings utilizing velvet sofas in deep burgundy, paired with brass-accented coffee tables to foster an intimate, celebratory atmosphere.

Floral Direction

Lush, cascading installations focusing on rich, textural blooms. Primary floral elements will utilize deep reds and creams to contrast against the architectural gold elements.

Included Scope

  • Main Stage Backdrop & Flooring
  • Guest Lounge Furniture (for 150 pax)
  • Entrance Floral Archway
  • Ambient Lighting Package
